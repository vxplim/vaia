Install

Up and running in a minute.

  1. Download VAIA with the button at the top.
  2. Open the file and drag VAIA into Applications.
  3. Open VAIA. The first time, macOS asks you to confirm: go to System Settings → Privacy & Security and click Open Anyway.
Why does macOS ask?

Apple shows that message for apps that aren't registered with a paid Apple Developer account yet. VAIA's code is public on GitHub, so anyone can see exactly what it does.

Is it free? Where is my data?

Free and open source, with no ads, accounts or subscriptions. Tabs, Spaces and settings stay on your Mac, and passwords are locked in your Mac's Keychain.

Does Netflix work?

YouTube and most sites work. Netflix and a few other protected streaming services are coming in a future version.
